About IASM-WMS

The Integrated Ashram School Management and Welfare Monitoring System is designed to provide a unified digital platform for managing Ashram schools. It supports school administration, academic activities, student welfare and departmental monitoring through a centralized system.

System Guidelines

Users can access the system according to their assigned roles and responsibilities. School-level information and departmental monitoring will be controlled through appropriate access permissions.
